RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
(Sample lease written for the LeaseLens demo. All names are fictional.)

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on August 1, 2026, by and between Northgate Residential Partners LLC ("Landlord") and Maya Torres and Jordan Lee (each a "Tenant," together "Tenants") for the premises located at 1120 E. University Drive, Unit 3B (the "Premises"), a two-bedroom apartment in the Saguaro Commons community.

1. TERM
The term of this Lease begins on August 15, 2026 and ends at 11:59 p.m. on August 14, 2027.

2. RENT
Tenants shall pay monthly rent of $1,950.00, due on the first (1st) day of each month. Rent paid after 11:59 p.m. on the third (3rd) day of the month is late. A late fee of $75.00 will be charged on the fourth (4th) day, plus $15.00 per day thereafter until rent is paid in full.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Upon signing, Tenants shall pay a security deposit of $1,950.00. The deposit will be returned within the time required by law after Tenants vacate, less any amounts for unpaid rent, damages, or cleaning. A professional carpet cleaning charge of $225.00 will be deducted from the security deposit upon move-out regardless of the condition of the carpet. Tenants may request a move-in inspection checklist within five (5) days of move-in.

4. UTILITIES
Tenants are responsible for electricity, which shall be placed in a Tenant's name prior to move-in. Water, sewer, and trash services will be billed to Tenants monthly by Landlord's third-party billing provider using a ratio utility billing system based on occupancy and square footage, and such charges may vary from month to month. A monthly billing service fee of $4.95 applies.

5. COMMUNITY FEES
In addition to rent, Tenants agree to pay the following monthly fees: Amenity Fee $35.00; Valet Trash Service $25.00; Pest Control $6.00; Technology Package (internet and cable) $65.00. A one-time, non-refundable Administrative Fee of $150.00 is due at signing. Reserved parking, if available, is $50.00 per month per space.

6. OCCUPANTS AND JOINT LIABILITY
The Premises shall be occupied only by the Tenants named above. Each Tenant is jointly and severally liable for all obligations under this Lease, including the full amount of rent and any damages caused by any Tenant, occupant, or guest, regardless of any separate agreement among Tenants regarding how rent is divided.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Landlord shall maintain the structural elements, roof, and major building systems of the Premises. Tenants shall promptly report all maintenance needs through the resident portal. Tenants shall be responsible for the cost of all repairs to the Premises up to $150.00 per occurrence, including repairs to appliances, plumbing fixtures, and HVAC filters, regardless of cause. Landlord may enter the Premises to make repairs upon twenty-four (24) hours' notice, or at any time in an emergency.

8. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING
Tenants shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises. Any attempted assignment or sublease shall be void and shall constitute a material default of this Lease.

9. EARLY TERMINATION
If Tenants vacate the Premises before the end of the term, Tenants shall remain liable for all rent due for the remainder of the term, and all such rent shall become immediately due and payable. In addition, Tenants shall pay a lease break fee equal to one (1) month's rent. Landlord is not required to re-rent the Premises before seeking payment from Tenants.

10. RENEWAL
This Lease shall automatically renew for an additional twelve (12) month term unless either party delivers written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term. Notice from Tenants must be delivered by certified mail to Landlord's office. Upon renewal, monthly rent may be increased by Landlord upon thirty (30) days' notice.

11. PETS
No animals of any kind are permitted on the Premises without a signed Pet Addendum and payment of a non-refundable pet fee of $300.00 and monthly pet rent of $35.00.

12. ALTERATIONS
Tenants shall not paint, install fixtures, or make alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent.

13. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Lease, together with the Community Policies and any addenda signed by the parties, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
