# Clause checklist

Each entry: `clause_type` id, what to look for, and how to set `risk_level`.

## security_deposit
Amount, where it's held, deadline for return, allowed deductions, move-out inspection.
- **low**: ≤ 1 month's rent, clear return deadline, itemized deductions, move-in checklist.
- **medium**: no return deadline stated, or vague "cleaning" deductions.
- **high**: non-refundable deposit, automatic deductions (e.g., "carpet cleaning fee will be deducted"), deposit > 1.5× rent.

## early_termination
What happens if the tenant needs to leave before the lease ends.
- **low**: fixed buy-out ≤ 1 month's rent, or landlord must try to re-rent.
- **medium**: tenant owes rent until the unit is re-rented.
- **high**: tenant owes all remaining rent for the full term, plus fees ("acceleration" clause).

## auto_renewal
Whether the lease renews automatically and how much notice is needed to leave.
- **low**: converts to month-to-month, ≤ 30 days' notice.
- **medium**: renews automatically, 30–45 days' written notice.
- **high**: renews for a full new term, ≥ 60 days' notice, or notice must be in a specific form (certified mail).

## utilities
Which utilities (electric, gas, water, sewer, trash, internet) the tenant pays, and how they're billed.
- **low**: clearly listed; tenant pays providers directly.
- **medium**: "ratio utility billing" (RUBS) or shared meters split by formula.
- **high**: tenant pays "all utilities" with no list, or landlord bills a flat amount that can change.

## extra_fees
Amenity, admin, application, pest control, trash valet, parking, technology, late fees.
- **low**: none beyond a reasonable late fee.
- **medium**: one or two monthly fees under ~$50 total.
- **high**: monthly fees over ~$50 total, or late fees that grow daily without a cap.

## roommate_liability
"Joint and several" liability: whether one roommate can be charged the *entire* rent if another doesn't pay or moves out.
- **low**: individual (by-the-bed) leases; each tenant owes only their share.
- **medium**: joint liability mentioned, but replacement roommate process exists.
- **high**: each tenant is liable for the full rent and all damages caused by any occupant.

## subletting
Whether the tenant can sublet or assign the lease (important for summer or study abroad).
- **low**: allowed with landlord approval "not unreasonably withheld."
- **medium**: allowed only with written consent and a fee.
- **high**: prohibited entirely, or subletting is grounds for termination.

## maintenance
Who handles repairs, response times, and what the tenant must pay for.
- **low**: landlord handles repairs; tenant pays only for damage they cause.
- **medium**: tenant responsible for minor repairs or appliances up to a dollar amount.
- **high**: tenant pays for all repairs below a high threshold (e.g., $200), or for "normal wear and tear."
